# Mergington High School Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Extra-Curricular Activities project
welcoming, respectful, and free of harassment for everyone in the school community.

## Our Standards

Positive behavior includes welcoming language, respect for different viewpoints,
constructive criticism, empathy, and a focus on the students and school community.

Unacceptable behavior includes inappropriate language or imagery, trolling,
personal attacks, harassment, and publishing private information without permission.

## Responsibilities

Project maintainers clarify and fairly enforce these standards. They may remove,
edit, or reject contributions that do not follow this code of conduct.

## Scope

These standards apply in project spaces and when representing the project or
school publicly, including official accounts and appointed representatives.

## Enforcement

In the exercise's school scenario, concerns may be reported to the IT Club faculty
advisor. Complaints should be reviewed promptly and fairly. Violations may lead
to temporary or permanent restrictions on participation.
